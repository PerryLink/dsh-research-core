## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-08

First release: REP v1 is frozen. Any field change from here on requires
`schemaVersion: 2` plus a migration; additive, optional fields are the only
non-breaking evolution.

### Added

- **REP v1 JSON Schema** (`schema/rep-v1.schema.json`, 2020-12) for
  `SourceRef`, `Snapshot`, `Anchor`, `Evidence`, `Claim`, `CheckResult`,
  `Artifact`, `AuditEvent`, `ReportManifest` and the `ResearchRecord`
  interchange container.
- **TypeScript types** mirroring the schema 1:1.
- **Deterministic primitives**: `sha256Hex`, `snapshotIdOf`, `objectPathOf`,
  `hashRefHex`, `stableId`, `canonicalJson` (JCS-compatible), `sha256Json`.
- **Claim evaluation** (`evaluateClaim`) with five documented verdict paths and
  a conservative mapping (absent number → `insufficient`, absent quote →
  `disproven`, broken snapshot → `contradicted`).
- **Invariant validation** (`validateRep`) covering cross-references, content
  addressing, verdict legality, `not_found` bookkeeping, the audit chain and the
  manifest seal.
- **Record verification** (`verifyRecord`, `verifyRecordFile`) that re-hashes
  every snapshot and re-locates every quoted anchor; a mismatch is a hard
  failure, never repaired.
- **Manifest sealing** (`buildManifest`, `sealManifest`, `verifyManifestSeal`)
  with derived evidence status where `not_found` can never be `ok`.
- **Hash-chained audit events** (`createAuditEvent`, `verifyAuditChain`).
- **SARIF 2.1.0 export** (`toSarif`, `validationToSarif`).
- **Content-addressed local store** (`EvidenceStore`): immutable objects,
  append-only JSONL journals, latest-wins checks/verdicts, sealed records,
  tamper detection, refuse-to-overwrite guarantees.
- **Cordis service** `ctx.researchEvidence` + `LocalResearchEvidenceService`,
  with an optional structural bridge to `ctx.researchReport`
  (`attachReport`, `importReportEvidence`, `mirrorToReport`).
- **CLI** `dsh-rep-verify` with human, `--json` and `--sarif` output and
  `0/1/2` exit codes.
- **20 golden fixtures** (3 valid, 17 single-cause invalid) generated by the
  real functions and gated against drift.
- **107 tests** across 10 files; `typecheck`, `test`, `build` green.

### Notes

- The package registers no model-facing tools; it is the protocol/service layer
  for the 18 research plugins.
- Runtime dependencies: zero.
- `not_found` is documented as neither evidence of fabrication nor evidence of
  cleanliness, enforced at the schema, validator and store layers.
